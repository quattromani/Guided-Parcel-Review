# Property Record Reconciliation Caveat

## Working brief

Guided Parcel Review must reproduce the latest published property record without silently correcting or inferring county data. A changed assessed value does not establish that every related technical field was reconciled across the assessor's systems.

Until the assessor's internal workflow is confirmed, every loaded parcel review carries a restrained status notice immediately below the guided menu. The notice scrolls with the page rather than occupying persistent navigation space. It tells taxpayers that valuation review, protest, or equalization may affect a current value even when every related public-record detail has not been updated, then directs them to review and mark incorrect facts.

The public card remains the authority for what the interface displays. Earlier same-year cards are preserved as immutable source snapshots so future revision-history work can distinguish annual changes from revisions within one assessment year. The caveat is a global working assumption, not a finding that any particular field on every parcel is wrong.

## Review trigger

Revisit this policy after the assessor's office confirms how valuation, protest, equalization, and technical property-detail corrections move between systems. At that point the notice may remain global, become conditional, or be replaced by a more precise record-status model.
