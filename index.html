You have reviewed how this property compares with similar properties nearby. Next, step back and look at how assessments are evaluated across the county.

Each year, the county assessor submits an assessment abstract to the state for equalization review before valuation notices are mailed. During that process, qualified property sales from the study period are compared to assessed values to produce the statistical measures below.

Each measure highlights a different part of the assessment process. Viewed together, they help show how consistently and proportionately assessments are performing over time.

Assessment places a property within the shared value base.

Equalization checks that the value base remains uniform and proportionate before levies and taxes are applied.
